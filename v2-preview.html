Scope

One job, not another all-in-one PDF suite.

Built for

  • Supplier catalogs
  • SKU and price tables
  • Quotations
  • Multi-page tables
  • PDFs that still require manual verification

Not built for

  • Merge PDF
  • Split PDF
  • Sign PDF
  • Watermark
  • General PDF editing and Word conversion
