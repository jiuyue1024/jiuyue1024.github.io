The problem

The dangerous conversion is the one that looks successful.

Obvious failures are easy to catch. Silent structural errors are not: shifted rows, repeated headers, broken cross-page tables, wrapped text split into the wrong cell, or a value that quietly lands under the wrong SKU.

“Having to hunt for silent layout errors is what ruins the whole experience.”

That feedback changed the product direction. PDflow v2 is being built around reducing verification work — not around becoming another PDF toolbox.

What deserves a second look
  • Multi-page table continuity
  • Wrapped text and row shifts
  • Repeated headers treated as data
  • Missing or uncertain values
  • Source / extracted value mismatches
The workflow

Conversion is only the middle step.

The goal is a reviewable result with provenance, not just another XLSX file.

Scope

One job, not another all-in-one PDF suite.

Built for

People repeatedly turning supplier catalogs, SKU lists, quotations, and difficult business PDFs into structured Excel — and still checking whether the result is trustworthy.

Not built for

If you mainly need merge, split, watermark, PDF editing, signing, or Word conversion, PDflow v2 is intentionally not trying to replace a full PDF suite.
