1. Online Beta Conversion

When you choose Try Online Beta, your PDF is sent to PDflow's hosted conversion worker solely to run the current web-safe conversion routes. No account or email address is required for this conversion flow. Please do not upload confidential, regulated, or otherwise sensitive documents.

2. Online Beta Retention and Deletion

The conversion worker deletes its input PDF when processing finishes. If an Excel file is produced, that temporary Excel file is deleted after the first successful download or automatically after 30 minutes, whichever comes first. Job-status metadata such as the filename, result state, and review summary may remain temporarily in worker memory but is not intended as persistent document storage.

3. Manual Sample Submissions

If you use the separate sample-submission form on yinliupdflow.com, the PDF, your email address, and the description you provide are used for PDflow testing and evaluation. The PDF is uploaded to private cloud storage and forwarded as an attachment to the PDflow research mailbox. This flow does not currently promise a fixed retention period or complete deletion across every copy. Please redact confidential or sensitive information before submitting a sample.

4. Website Analytics

This website may use privacy-focused analytics to understand aggregate page views and navigation patterns. Analytics are separate from uploaded PDF contents and are not used to inspect document contents.

5. Infrastructure and Operational Logs

Hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard connection and operational metadata needed to deliver the website and conversion service. PDflow's application code does not intentionally log PDF contents. The Online Beta is designed for low-volume testing and should not be treated as secure archival storage.

6. Children

PDflow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Online Beta or sample-submission flow.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new "Last updated" date on this page.
