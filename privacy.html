Privacy Policy

Local-first by default.
Website samples are opt-in.

The PDflow desktop app processes files locally. If you explicitly submit a PDF sample through this website, the sample is uploaded to private cloud storage and forwarded to the PDflow research mailbox for testing and evaluation.

1. Desktop App Processing

The PDflow desktop app runs on your computer and does not require an account. Files processed in the desktop app stay on your device unless you separately choose to submit a sample through the PDflow website.

2. Website Sample Submissions

If you choose to submit a PDF sample through yinliupdflow.com, the PDF, your email address, and the description you provide are processed for PDflow testing and evaluation. The PDF is uploaded to private cloud storage and forwarded as an attachment to the PDflow research mailbox. Sample submission is optional. Please remove or redact confidential or sensitive information before uploading.

3. Website Analytics

This website may use privacy-focused analytics to understand aggregate page views and navigation patterns. Analytics are separate from submitted PDF samples and are not used to inspect document contents.

4. Downloads, Updates & Crash Reports

Downloads and update checks may connect to GitHub Releases and send standard browser or platform information required for those requests. The desktop app does not automatically upload your PDF contents as part of update checks. We do not automatically collect crash reports, error logs, or stack traces.

5. Retention and Deletion

Submitted PDF samples may exist in both private cloud storage and the PDflow research mailbox. We do not currently publish a fixed retention period or promise complete deletion across all copies. Please redact sensitive information before submitting a sample.

6. Children

PDflow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is a tool, not a service — there is no signup and no profile.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be announced on the blog and noted by a new "Last updated" date. Continued use after a change indicates acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy or a submitted sample? Email 1950588085@qq.com or contact the PDflow project maintainer.
