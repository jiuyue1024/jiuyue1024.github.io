About

Built for designers.
Powered by local-first.

PDflow is a free, open-source document platform that respects your privacy. Every operation runs on your machine — no cloud, no tracking, no signup.

Our Mission

Most PDF tools are bloated, expensive, and upload your sensitive documents to the cloud. We built PDflow to fix that. Local-first means your data never leaves your machine — and you don't need an internet connection to use the app.

Built With

PySide6 (Qt 6.11+) for the UI, PyMuPDF for PDF processing, RapidOCR for scanned documents, reportlab for template rendering. All open-source, all fast, all private.

Open Source

PDflow is open source under the MIT license. View the source on GitHub, submit issues, or contribute. The community decides what gets built next.

Roadmap

v1.1: 6+ templates (current) · v1.2: layout engine (current) · v1.3: visual canvas editor · v2.0: PDflow Studio. Read the full roadmap on GitHub.
